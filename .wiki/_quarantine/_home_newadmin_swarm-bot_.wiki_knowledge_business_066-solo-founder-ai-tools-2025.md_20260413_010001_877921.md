## Core Knowledge

### The 7 AI Tool Categories for Solo Founders

1. **Trend Radar**
   - Live web research surfacing emerging opportunities
   - Smart comment replies to ride trending conversations
   - Tool: Perplexity, Semantic Scholar, Brandwatch

2. **Competitor X-Ray**
   - Spot gaps in your niche without expensive tools
   - Reverse-engineer winning offers
   - Tool: ChatGPT + web browsing, Similarweb free tier

3. **Autopilot Architect**
   - Connect calendar, content, email into automated workflows
   - Run automatically while working—or sleeping
   - Tool: Make.com, Zapier, n8n

4. **Deck-in-a-Minute**
   - Turn outline/blog post into sales deck
   - No design skills required
   - Tool: Gamma, Tome, Beautiful.ai

5. **Audience Pulse**
   - Scrape top posts to learn what resonates
   - Repurpose content in your own voice
   - Tool: BuzzSumo, Curata, Semrush

6. **Numbers Translator**
   - Convert messy spreadsheets to clear dashboards
   - Decisions stop feeling like guesses
   - Tool: Claude, GPT-4o, Google Sheets + AI

7. **Solo Stack 2025**
   - Voice + scripting + strategy combination
   - Single founder = what used to need a whole team
   - Core: ChatGPT/Claude for strategy, specific tools for execution

## Edge Cases and Common Mistakes

3. **Ignoring automation**: Manual processes don't scale
4. **No workflow design**: Connect tools into chains, not isolated use
5. **Skipping strategy**: AI executes well, but humans must choose direction
