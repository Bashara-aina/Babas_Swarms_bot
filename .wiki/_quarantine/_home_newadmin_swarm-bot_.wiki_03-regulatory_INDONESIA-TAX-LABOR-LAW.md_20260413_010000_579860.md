## PPh21 Quick Reference (2026)

### Progressive Brackets
| PKP Annual | Rate |
|-----------|------|
| ≤ IDR 60,000,000 | 5% |
| IDR 60M–250M | 15% |
| IDR 250M–500M | 25% |
| IDR 500M–5B | 30% |
| > IDR 5B | 35% |

### PTKP Values (frozen PMK 66/2023)
TK/0: IDR 54M | K/0: IDR 58.5M | K/1: IDR 63M | K/2: IDR 67.5M | K/3: IDR 72M

### Non-Taxable (Natura) per PMK 66/2023
- Meals in kind ≤ IDR 1,000,000/month
- Transport in kind ≤ IDR 1,000,000/month
- Must be ACTUAL facility, NOT cash reimbursement

## BPJS Quick Reference (2026)

### Ketenagakerjaan
JHT: Employee 2% + Employer 3.7%
JP: Employee 1% + Employer 2% (cap IDR 9,559,600/month)
JKK: Employer only, 0.24%–1.74% by risk class
JKM: Employer only 0.3%

### Kesehatan
Employee: 1% (cap IDR 12,000,000/month)
Employer: 4% (+1%/child max 5 children) (cap IDR 12,000,000/month)

## THR (Tunjangan Hari Raya) Rules

Per UU 13/2003 + SE Kemnaker:
- Halalbihalal: 1 month salary (if >12 months tenure)
- Pro-rata for <12 months: (tenure_months / 12) × monthly_salary
- Paid at minimum 7 days before Hari Raya
- Failure = criminal violation (fines + imprisonment for employer)

## Overtime Rules (UU 13/2003)
- Hour 1: 1.5× hourly rate
- Hour 2–4: 2× hourly rate
- Rest day / National holiday: 2×–3× depending on conditions
