# Distribution > Product — The Clay/Relay Pattern

Source: Glean playbook + Clay.com + Relay growth case studies

## The Counterintuitive Truth
"A mediocre product with great distribution beats a great product
with no distribution. Every time."

## The Clay Distribution Playbook
1. Build vertical templates that solve one workflow extremely well
2. Find influencers IN that vertical (not tech influencers — domain influencers)
3. Give them templates for free → they share with their audience
4. Each share = qualified leads who already understand the pain
5. Scale: add more templates, more verticals, more influencers

## The Freemium Skill Pattern
- Free version → builds distribution + reviews + trust
- Paid version → advanced features for serious users
- Custom builds → $500–2000 for enterprise
- Community → self-reinforcing as users become advocates

## Applied to cekwajar.id
- Free tool: basic PPh 21 calculator (drives organic traffic)
- Paid tool: full salary audit + BPJS + compliance report
- Influencers: Indonesian HR Twitter/LinkedIn accounts
- Template: "salary structure template for 50-person company"
- Community: HR managers who found value → share with peers

## Applied to rumahlabuh.com
- Free: property value estimator in Koto/Jakarta
- Paid: full rental yield analysis + tenant screening
- Influencers: Indonesian real estate agents on Instagram
- Templates: landlord contract templates

## Rule
Build the free tool that makes your paid tool obvious.
The free tool is not charity. It is the best marketing you can do.
