## ADR LOG

### ADR-001: Circuit Breaker Design
**Decision:** Implement per-component circuit breakers (LLM, search, browser) with 3 states (CLOSED/OPEN/HALF_OPEN)  
**Consequences:** Adds complexity to LLM call path; requires careful timeout tuning  
**File:** `core/circuit_breaker.py`

### ADR-002: Async Task Error Handling Policy  
**Decision:** All `asyncio.create_task()` calls must have exception handling via done_callback or wrapped in try/except  
**Consequences:** More verbose code; better debuggability  
**Files:** Throughout codebase

### ADR-003: Wiki Auto-Ingest Quality Gates
**Decision:** Reject ingested pages with < 3 sentences or < 100 chars; skip exact duplicate hashes  
**Consequences:** Cleaner knowledge base; possible false positives on short legitimate content  
**File:** `core/wiki_quality_gate.py`
