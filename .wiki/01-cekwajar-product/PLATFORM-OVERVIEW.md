# cekwajar.id — Platform Overview

## The Core Proposition

cekwajar.id answers one question for Indonesian users: **"Apakah ini wajar?"**
(Is this fair?)

| Tool | Question | Primary User | Pain Point Solved |
|------|----------|-------------|------------------|
| **Wajar Gaji** | Is my salary fair? | Employee | No transparent salary data in Indonesia |
| **Wajar Slip** | Is my payslip correct & legal? | Employee | Complex PPh21/BPJS many HR departments get wrong |
| **Wajar Kabur** | Should I work abroad? | Mid-career professional | No PPP-adjusted comparison for Indonesian workers |
| **Wajar Tanah** | Is this land price fair? | Property buyer | NJOP severely undervalues market; no transparent benchmark |
| **Wajar Hidup** | How much to live here? | Job seeker/relocator | No reliable city-level CoL for Indonesian cities |
