- Self-taught coder, previous startup that FAILED
- Started Medvi from his home in Los Angeles

## The 3-Layer Operational Architecture

```
LAYER 1: Customer Interface (Gallagher BUILT)
  ├── Landing page + marketing + paid ads (AI-generated)
  ├── Patient health intake form
  ├── Payment processing (Stripe subscriptions ~$297/month)
  ├── Patient portal (treatment instructions, refills, support)
  └── Customer service (AI: ElevenLabs voice + Grok text)

LAYER 2: Medical Backend (OUTSOURCED → CareValidate)
  ├── Licensed physician network (all 50 US states)
  ├── Async telehealth review of intake forms
  ├── Prescription eligibility determination
  ├── HIPAA compliance infrastructure
  └── State medical board licensing management

LAYER 3: Pharmacy Fulfillment (OUTSOURCED → OpenLoop Health)
  ├── Compounding pharmacy network (US-certified)
  ├── Semaglutide + tirzepatide compounding
  ├── Quality testing of medications
  ├── Cold-chain shipping to patients
  └── Refill management + patient tracking
```

## The Customer Journey

1. Customer sees AI-generated ad (Midjourney/Runway)
2. Clicks to en-medvi.com landing page
3. Fills health questionnaire (BMI, conditions, goals)
4. Pays subscription ($297–397/month or $99 first month offer)
5. Form routed to CareValidate physician (async, 24–48 hours)
6. If approved → prescription sent to compounding pharmacy
7. Medication shipped to home (1–3 day delivery)
8. Patient accesses portal for instructions + support
9. Monthly auto-renew

## The FDA Warning (Regulatory Risk Lesson)

March 2026: FDA warning letters to 30+ telehealth companies.
Medvi scrutinized for:
- Marketing compounded semaglutide as equivalent to Ozempic
- Drug shortage exemption becoming invalid as Novo Nordisk increased supply
- Unverified efficacy claims

**KEY LESSON:** Gallagher's window was partly TEMPORARY (drug shortage).
He built fast DURING the window. Now pivoting to men's/women's health
using the same infrastructure (same playbook, new market).

Bashara's window (Indonesian labor law complexity) is PERMANENT.
This is structurally BETTER than Gallagher's position.
