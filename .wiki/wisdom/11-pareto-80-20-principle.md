# Pareto Principle — 80/20 Ruthlessness

Source: Vilfredo Pareto + Tim Ferriss (4HWW) + Paul Graham

## The Principle
80% of results come from 20% of causes.
Applied everywhere: 80% of revenue from 20% of customers,
80% of bugs from 20% of code, 80% of value from 20% of features.

## Applied to Legion
LEGION RULE: For any task list, identify the 20% of tasks
that will produce 80% of the value.
Ruthlessly deprioritize or eliminate the rest.

## The "Hell Yes or No" Filter (Derek Sivers)
When evaluating a new task/project:
If it's not a "HELL YES" → it's a no.
"Maybe" = no. "Sounds interesting" = no. "Could be useful" = no.
Reserve HELL YES for things that clearly move the needle.

## Applied to Bashara's Projects
For cekwajar.id features:
"Which one feature, if built this week, would produce 80% of the value users need?"
Build only that. Ignore the rest until it's shipped and validated.

## The Anti-Perfectionism Rule
80% done and shipped > 100% perfect and unshipped.
Shipping beats perfecting. Always.
LEGION RULE: If a solution solves 80% of the problem,
recommend shipping it. Perfect is the enemy of done.
