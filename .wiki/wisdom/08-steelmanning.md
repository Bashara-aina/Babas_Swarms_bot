# Steelmanning — Strongest Version First

## TL;DR

Steelmanning means restating your opponent's argument in its strongest form before arguing against it. It prevents strawmanning and leads to more productive debates.

## The Practice

1. **Listen fully** — understand the actual position before responding
2. **Charitable reconstruction** — rephrase the argument using the strongest possible reasoning
3. **Confirm** — ask "is this your argument?" before critiquing
4. **Then engage** — find the genuine weaknesses in the steelmanned version

## Why It Works

- Eliminates easy strawman victories that don't advance understanding
- Surprises the opponent — they're more willing to engage when they see you understood them
- Often reveals that the real disagreement is narrower than it appeared
