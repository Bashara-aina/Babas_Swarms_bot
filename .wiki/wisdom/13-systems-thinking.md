# Systems Thinking

## TL;DR

Look for feedback loops, leverage points, and unintended consequences. Systems often have counterintuitive behavior that only becomes clear when you map the full structure.

## Key Concepts

- **Feedback loops** — positive (reinforcing) and negative (balancing) loops
- **Delays** — systems with long delays between action and consequence are harder to control
- **Emergence** — system-level behavior that can't be predicted from individual components
