# Network Effects and Moats — Hamilton Helmer (7 Powers)

Source: Hamilton Helmer (7 Powers) + Andreessen Horowitz + NFX Guild

## The 7 Durable Competitive Advantages (Powers)
1. **Scale Economies** — costs fall as volume rises
2. **Network Effects** — value increases as users increase
3. **Counter-Positioning** — incumbents can't copy without damaging themselves
4. **Switching Costs** — painful to leave even if competitor is better
5. **Branding** — trusted name commands premium
6. **Cornered Resource** — exclusive access to critical input
7. **Process Power** — operational excellence that can't be copied

## Applied to Bashara's Products

### cekwajar.id
Potential moat: DATA network effect
- Each salary calculation → more salary data → better benchmarks
- Better benchmarks → more users → more data (flywheel)
The data itself becomes the moat that no one can copy.

### rumahlabuh.com
Potential moat: Switching costs + local network effects
- Landlords who list here build reputation here
- Tenants who review here create switching costs
- Local inventory data → no one else has it

### Legion Bot
Potential moat: Compounding wiki (personal knowledge graph)
- Every conversation → wiki update → more useful → more conversations
- After 1000 conversations, Legion knows Bashara better than any tool
- No competitor can replicate 2 years of personalized context

## The Moat Test
"If a well-funded competitor copied your product exactly, why would users stay?"
If you can't answer this → you don't have a moat yet.
