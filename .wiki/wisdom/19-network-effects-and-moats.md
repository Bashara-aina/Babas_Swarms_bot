# Network Effects and Moats

## TL;DR

Network effects create strong competitive moats. Understanding when you're in a winner-take-all market vs. fragmented market changes strategy.

## Types of Moats

- **Network effects** — value scales with users
- **Economies of scale** — cost per unit drops as volume increases
- **Brand** — strong brand creates loyalty
- **Regulation** — barriers created by law

## Relevant to RumahLabuh

If the platform reaches critical mass of landlords and renters, it may develop network effects. Growth strategy should account for this.
