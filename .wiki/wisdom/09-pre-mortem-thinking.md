# Pre-Mortem Thinking

## TL;DR

Before starting a project, imagine it has failed spectacularly. Identify what could have caused the failure to proactively mitigate those risks.

## How It Works

1. **Picture failure** — vividly imagine the project has catastrophically failed
2. **Ask "why?"** — what specific causes led to this failure?
3. **Identify risks** — translate causes into actionable mitigations
4. **Assign owners** — each risk gets a mitigation plan and owner

## Connection to Steelmanning

Pre-mortem is steelmanning applied to failure scenarios — it forces consideration of the strongest counterarguments to your plan before you've committed.
