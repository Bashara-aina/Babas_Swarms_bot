# Regret Minimization Framework

## TL;DR

Project yourself to the end of your life. Which choice produces less regret? This cuts through short-term anxiety and clarifies what matters.

## Why It Works

Most big decisions paralyze us in the moment. The regret framework removes the decision from the present anxiety and places it in a lifetime perspective.

## Application

Used by Jeff Bezos for Amazon, Sam Altman for OpenAI. For major career or startup decisions: "will I regret not trying this?"
