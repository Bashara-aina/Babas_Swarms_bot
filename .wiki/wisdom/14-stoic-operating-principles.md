# Stoic Operating Principles

## TL;DR

Stoicism teaches: (1) separate what's in your control from what isn't, (2) treat obstacles as fuel, (3) maintain inner stability regardless of external chaos.

## Core Practices

1. **Control dichotomy** — focus energy only on what's actually controllable
2. **Negative visualization** — mentally prepare for losses to reduce their impact
3. **Objective judgment** — describe events factually without emotional coloring

## In Technical Work

Use stoic framing when debugging: "the bug exists independent of how I feel about it" — focus on fixing, not frustration.
