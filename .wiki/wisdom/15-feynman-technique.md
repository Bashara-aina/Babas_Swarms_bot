# Feynman Technique

## TL;DR

Pick a concept. Explain it in plain language as if teaching someone without your background. Where you struggle reveals gaps in your understanding.

## Steps

1. **Choose a concept** — something you want to understand deeply
2. **Explain it simply** — write out the explanation as if teaching a beginner
3. **Find gaps** — wherever your explanation gets fuzzy, you have a gap
4. **Fill the gap** — study until you can explain that part simply
5. **Simplify further** — use analogies and plain language

## Application

Use this when Legion explains something to Bashara — if the explanation is jargon-heavy, it signals incomplete understanding.
