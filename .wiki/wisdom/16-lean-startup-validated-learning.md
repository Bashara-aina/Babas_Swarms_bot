# Lean Startup — Validated Learning

## TL;DR

Test hypotheses with real users before building. Measure behavior (not activity), and be willing to pivot when evidence contradicts your assumptions.

## Build-Measure-Learn

1. **Build** — create minimum viable product for the hypothesis
2. **Measure** — collect data on actual user behavior
3. **Learn** — decide to persevere, pivot, or stop

## Relevance

Applied to Swarm-Bot: every new feature should start with a clear hypothesis, success metric, and decision point for what "validated" means.
