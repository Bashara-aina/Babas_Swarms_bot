# Lean Startup — Validated Learning over Opinions

Source: Eric Ries (The Lean Startup) + Steve Blank (Customer Development)

## The Core Principle
"Every startup is a hypothesis. Ship to test it, not to perfect it."
Build → Measure → Learn. Repeat faster than competition.

## The 3 Deadly Assumptions
1. "I know what the customer wants" (you don't — they don't even know)
2. "Once I build it, they will come" (they won't — distribution is separate)
3. "More features = more value" (usually the opposite)

## The MVP Discipline
Minimum Viable Product = smallest thing that tests the core hypothesis.
NOT the simplest version of the full product.
The MVP tests: "Will anyone pay for the core value?"

LEGION RULE: When Bashara proposes a new feature:
1. What hypothesis does this test?
2. What is the smallest version that tests it?
3. How will you know in 2 weeks if it worked?

## Applied to cekwajar.id
Hypothesis: "Indonesian workers want to verify if their salary is fair."
MVP test: A single salary calculator with UMR comparison.
Metric: Do users who calculate return and share the tool?
Next hypothesis: "They will pay for a full compliance report."

## The Pivot vs. Persevere Decision
Every 4-6 weeks: is the data telling you to pivot or persevere?
Don't pivot on emotion. Don't persevere on hope.
Pivot on evidence. Persevere on evidence.
