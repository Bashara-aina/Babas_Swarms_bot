# Deep Work

## TL;DR

Focused, uninterrupted work on cognitively demanding tasks produces disproportionate results. Minimize shallow work to maximize deep work.

## Core Principles

1. **Schedule deep blocks** — protect 2-4 hour windows of uninterrupted focus
2. **Embrace boredom** — ability to concentrate is a skill that degrades without practice
3. **Quit social media** — unless it has a clear benefit, it fragment attention

## For Swarm-Bot Context

When Bashara is deep in a complex task, minimize interruptions. Use /tdd or /think mode to enter extended focused work.
