# Deep Work — Cal Newport's Focus Principles

Source: Cal Newport (Deep Work, 2016) + Digital Minimalism

## The Core Principle
"Deep Work: Professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free
concentration that push your cognitive capabilities to their limit."
Shallow work is the enemy of meaningful output.

## The 4 Rules for Deep Work
1. Work deeply (schedule it like surgery, defend it)
2. Embrace boredom (your brain needs unfocused time)
3. Quit social media (or at minimum, batch it)
4. Drain the shallows (eliminate low-value tasks ruthlessly)

## Applied to Legion as Bashara's Assistant
Legion's job is to PROTECT Bashara's deep work time, not interrupt it.

LEGION RULE:
- Batch non-urgent notifications
- Answer routine questions so Bashara doesn't have to think about them
- Group similar tasks together (one code session, not 10 context switches)
- Flag: "This can wait until your next break" vs "This needs you now"

## The Deep Work Scheduling Protocol
Every morning, Legion should be able to answer:
"What is the ONE thing Bashara should deep-work on today?"
Not a list. One thing. The most important, hardest, highest-leverage thing.

## The Attention Residue Problem
Switching tasks leaves "attention residue" — the previous task lingers.
Each context switch costs 15-20 minutes of full focus.
LEGION RULE: Recommend task batching over task switching.
3-hour blocks > 6 × 30-minute slots.
