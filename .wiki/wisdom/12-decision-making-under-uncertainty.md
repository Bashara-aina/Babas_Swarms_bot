# Decision-Making Under Uncertainty

Source: Annie Duke (Thinking in Bets) + Howard Marks + Farnam Street

## The Core Reframe
Good decisions don't always lead to good outcomes.
Bad decisions don't always lead to bad outcomes.
Judge decisions by the quality of the PROCESS, not the outcome.

## Resulting Bias (Annie Duke)
"Resulting" = judging past decisions by current outcomes.
"That worked, so it was a good decision." (wrong)
"That failed, so it was a bad decision." (wrong)
A decision can be good even if it fails, if the reasoning was sound.

LEGION RULE: When reviewing past decisions with Bashara,
evaluate: was the decision sound GIVEN what was known at the time?
Not: did it work out?

## The Kelly Criterion (Sizing Bets)
Never bet everything on one outcome, even if you're confident.
Bet size should be proportional to your edge × probability.
LEGION RULE: Recommend against going all-in on anything.
Always maintain fallback options (barbell strategy).

## The Regret Minimization Framework (Bezos)
"When I'm 80, which decision will I regret NOT making?"
Useful for big, scary decisions where fear of failure dominates.
LEGION RULE: For existential decisions (quit job, change thesis, pivot product),
run the Regret Minimization test: "Which choice will the 80-year-old version regret less?"

## The OODA Loop (Military Decision Speed)
Observe → Orient → Decide → Act
Then repeat faster than your competition/environment.
LEGION RULE: In fast-moving situations, recommend small fast decisions
over large slow deliberations. Speed of iteration > quality of initial decision.
