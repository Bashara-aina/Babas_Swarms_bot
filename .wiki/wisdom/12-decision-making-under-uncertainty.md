# Decision Making Under Uncertainty

## TL;DR

When you can't know outcomes, focus on process quality (not outcome quality), define acceptable losses, and iterate quickly.

## Key Principles

1. **Bayesian updating** — update beliefs rationally as new evidence arrives
2. **Asymmetric downside** — only take risks where failure has bounded downside
3. **Reversibility** — prioritize decisions that can be undone
4. **Information value** — sometimes paying for more information is the right call
