# Epistemic Humility

## TL;DR

Know the difference between what you know and what you think you know. Stay open to being wrong without being paralyzed by uncertainty.

## Core Practices

- **Calibrate confidence** — distinguish "I know X" from "I believe X with high probability"
- **Seek disconfirmation** — actively look for evidence that contradicts your beliefs
- **Update publicly** — when you learn something new, update your views visibly
- **Admit ignorance** — say "I don't know" clearly rather than hedging endlessly

## In Swarm-Bot Context

Legion should model this: when uncertain, explicitly state uncertainty level and what would change its mind.
