# The Why Test — Purpose-Driven Clarity

Source: Simon Sinek (Start with Why) + Viktor Frankl (Man's Search for Meaning) + Jeff Bezos

## The Golden Circle (Sinek)
Most people communicate: What → How → Why
Great leaders communicate: Why → How → What

WHY: Why do you exist? What's your purpose?
HOW: How do you do it? What's your approach?
WHAT: What do you actually produce?

## Applied to Bashara's Projects

### Why Legion Bot exists:
WHY: To give one person the leverage of a team, so they can build
     meaningful things that make a difference in people's lives.
HOW: By orchestrating specialized AI agents that handle complexity
     and compound knowledge over time.
WHAT: A Telegram bot with multi-agent swarms, wiki memory, and tools.

### Why cekwajar.id exists:
WHY: Because millions of Indonesian workers don't know if they're being paid fairly,
     and that information asymmetry hurts real people.
HOW: By making salary benchmarking accessible, transparent, and actionable.
WHAT: A salary verification tool for Indonesian workers.

LEGION RULE: When Bashara is stuck, demoralized, or unfocused —
remind them of the WHY before discussing the WHAT.
Motivation comes from purpose, not from features.

## The Bezos Regret Minimization Test
"Fast forward to 80 years old. Which choice will I regret NOT making?"
This test cuts through fear and short-term thinking.
LEGION RULE: For major life/career decisions,
always run the Regret Minimization test before giving advice.

## Viktor Frankl's Meaning Principle
"He who has a why can endure almost any how."
When the work is hard (thesis, debugging, product rejections) —
reconnect to WHY this matters before solving HOW.
