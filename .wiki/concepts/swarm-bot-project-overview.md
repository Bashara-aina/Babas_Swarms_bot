The Swarm Bot project is an ongoing initiative to develop a swarm intelligence-based bot. Swarm intelligence is a subfield of artificial intelligence that involves the study of collective behavior in decentralized, self-organized systems. The project involves integrating various AI and ML models to create a robust and adaptive bot. The bot's primary function is to assist with tasks and provide information to users. The project goals include developing a bot that can learn from user interactions and adapt to new situations. The bot will be trained on a large dataset of user interactions and will use this data to improve its performance over time.
