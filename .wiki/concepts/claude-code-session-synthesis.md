Claude-code sessions are designed to extract knowledge from large datasets. The process involves running prompts, auditing the results, and synthesizing the information into a structured knowledge base. This knowledge base can be used to inform decision-making and improve the effectiveness of tools like Legion Bot and Obsidian. By leveraging .wiki and Obsidian, users can create a comprehensive and easily accessible knowledge repository.
