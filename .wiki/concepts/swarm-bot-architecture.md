The Swarm Bot architecture is built upon a decentralized network of nodes, each with its own set of capabilities and resources. These nodes communicate with one another through a peer-to-peer protocol, enabling the system to adapt to changing environments and optimize task allocation. The architecture is designed to facilitate autonomous decision-making, allowing the bot to respond to new situations and challenges without human intervention.
