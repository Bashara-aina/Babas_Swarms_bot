Quantum entanglement is a fundamental concept in quantum mechanics that describes the correlation between two or more particles. When two particles are entangled, their properties become linked in such a way that the state of one particle cannot be described independently of the others. This phenomenon has been experimentally confirmed in various systems, including photons, electrons, and even large-scale objects like superconducting circuits. Entanglement is a key feature of quantum mechanics and has potential applications in quantum computing and cryptography.
