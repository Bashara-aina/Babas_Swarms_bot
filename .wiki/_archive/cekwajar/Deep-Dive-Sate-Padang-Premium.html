SECTION 1: SNAPSHOT SATE PADANG - Ringkasan Eksekutif & Verdict

Sate Padang Premium adalah BRAND PLAY bukan MARGIN PLAY. Ini adalah temuan paling kritis dalam analisis ini.

⚠️ THE HONEST TRUTH: Unit economics Sate Padang secara struktural lebih lemah dibanding Rice Bowl karena harga daging sapi di Jawa Tengah (Rp 131,000/kg - Rp 150,000/kg) membuat pure sapi sate menjadi break-even atau loss product pada harga kompetitif (20K-30K). Ini bukan masalah operasional, ini adalah masalah fundamental input cost.

Why Goyang Lidah Works at 15K (The Math Everyone Avoids)

Goyang Lidah sells 8 tusuk + kupat at Rp 15,000. Bagaimana mereka profit?

Teori 1 (Most Likely): Mereka menggunakan potongan daging SANGAT MURAH (tetelan, serat, mixed jeroan) dengan blended cost ~80-90K/kg, bukan beef premium. Jika per tusuk hanya 12-13g raw, maka 8 tusuk = 96-104g × 85K/kg = Rp 8,200-8,800 COGS meat saja, ditambah kupat, kuah, packaging mendekati Rp 15K dengan margin zero/breakeven.

Teori 2 (Volume Leverage): Mereka have supplier deals jauh di bawah market (bulk buying, langganan tetap), sehingga able to source lebih murah. Plausible untuk bisnis yang sudah mature.

Teori 3 (Subsidized by Margins Elsewhere): 15K adalah traffic builder (below-cost or breakeven), mereka profit dari jumbo (40K) dan upsize orders. Average order mungkin 22-25K.

Our Strategy (DIFERENSIASI): Instead of competing on 15K price point (impossible dengan beef quality), kami go PREMIUM positioning:
• Use Padang Panjang style (kuah kuning, gurih) bukan Pariaman (merah pedas) = visual differentiation
• 25-30K price tier (better margins)
• Mixed-quality approach: daging + lidah + jeroan in controlled ratios = acceptable for premium sate
• Target: 25+ porsi/night at 25K avg = 625K revenue/night

Market Context: Why Now, Why Solo

Pajang demographics: Predominantly student/young professionals (kost area near Universitas Sebelas Maret). This is Goyang Lidah's base market—price-sensitive, high frequency, repeat orders.

Laweyan positioning: More mixed (tourists, workers, some students). Better for premium/quality positioning.

Timing: Evening sate market is seasonally strong. Lebaran (April 2026) will see 20%+ price uplift on beef → margin compression for competitors → opportunity for early movers with cost structure locked in.

✓ Window of Opportunity: Launch in April-May (post-Lebaran stabilization) when beef prices normalize but customer habits from Lebaran surge remain sticky.

KUAH INGREDIENTS & RECIPE (VERIFIED)

Base Recipe (5 Liter Kuah untuk ~20-25 porsi):

Bumbu Halus (Grinding):
• 15 siung bawang merah
• 8 siung bawang putih
• 10 cabai merah (medium heat)
• 3cm kunyit segar
• 3cm jahe segar
• 2cm lengkuas
• 1 sdm ketumbar
• 1 sdt jinten

Teknik:
1. Grind semua bumbu dengan sedikit minyak sampai fine paste
2. Tumis bumbu paste dalam 100ml minyak goreng (8-10 min) sampai harum dan oil separates
3. Masukkan 4.5 liter kaldu daging (dari rebusan sate/bones)
4. Simmer 15-20 min
5. Mix 250g tepung beras dengan 500ml cold water (slurry), tuang sambil stirring
6. Masukkan 2 batang serai (bruised), 5 lembar daun jeruk, 2 lembar daun kunyit
7. Simmer 10 min, stir sampai thick dan glossy
8. Taste & adjust: salt (typically 2 tsp), add more kunyit paste if color needs deepening
9. Final consistency: should coat spoon, tidak terlalu thick (if too thick, thin dengan kaldu)

Shelf Life: 1-2 hari refrigerated. Kualitas DROP drastis setelah reheat berulang (tepung breaks down, separates).

Tumis bumbu paste dalam 100ml minyak (8-10 min) sampai harum dan oil separates. Masukkan kaldu (4.5L), simmer 15 min. Mix tepung beras dengan water (slurry), tuang sambil stirring. Add herbs (serai, daun jeruk, daun kunyit). Simmer 10 min sampai thick & glossy. Taste & adjust salt, kunyit color. Final: should coat spoon nicely, tidak terlalu kental. Cool dalam container, simpan di suhu room temp untuk late opening serving. 5L kuah ready (enough untuk ~20-25 porsi)
14:30-15:30 Prep Lontong, Kerupuk, Garnish Kitchen Staff (1) Portioning lontong jadi (1 piece per small container). Arrange kerupuk jangek dalam small bags/pouches (25g per unit). 15:30-16:30 Packaging Station Setup Asst/Picker (1) Arrange pada assembly line: containers (sate box, kuah cup, kerupuk pouch), lids, labels/stickers, plastic bags, napkins, instruction cards. Setup kuah warming station (if using water bath untuk light reheating). Prepare GoFood/GrabFood order list. Check temperature setup untuk kuah (can be chilled or light warm, depends on strategy). Station ready, all supplies within reach
16:30-22:00 SERVING WINDOW - GRILL & PACK Grill (1) + Packer (1) + Customer Service (1) CYCLE (repeat per order):
• Grill 8-10 tusuk per batch (4-6 min per side, depends on grill temp)
• Ladle kuah into sealed cup (250ml) → seal dengan cup sealer
• Place sate + lontong in primary container
• Add kerupuk pouch
• Wrap in plastic, add instruction card
• Label untuk GoFood/GrabFood order
• Queue untuk delivery pickup atau hand to customer

Bottleneck: GRILL. Single grill dapat handle 1 batch (8-10 tusuk) tiap 6-8 min = ~60-80 porsi dalam 5.5 hour window. Peak time: 17:30-20:00 (3-4 orders simultaneously = may need queue management).		 Completed orders, delivery-ready
22:00-22:30 Cleanup & Close All Staff Dispose remaining tusuk/meat, cool grill, clean grate/tools, wash containers & station, wipe down. Inventory remaining kuah (can be refrigerated for max 2 days if quality check OK). Kitchen cleaned, ready for next day

Critical Process Notes

⚠️ Grill Bottleneck: Single grill (80cm × 30cm) can only do ~60-80 porsi in 5.5 hours. If targeting 40+ porsi/night as base, you're at capacity. If aggressive (60+ porsi/night), MUST upgrade to double grill atau larger grill = capital cost ~Rp 500-800K additional.
Kuah Timing: Kuah dapat dibuat 2-3 jam before opening, disimpan at room temp (not cold). Jangan refrigerate immediately (akan terlalu thick when served). Customer dapat warm sendiri atau provide instruction untuk warming di rumah (included in box).
✓ Cost Saver: Lontong, kerupuk, bawang goreng = all bought jadi (ready-made). Don't try to make in-house pada tahap awal. Itu menambah work & overhead. Focus on quality daging + kuah (these are your differentiators).
