🥈 Runner-Up — Score 61/70

Premium Artisan Sambal & Bumbu Masak

Artisan sambal jars · Ready-to-cook paste · Bumbu rendang / opor / lodeh kits · TikTok-native product

Why This Is Compelling
  • Every Indonesian meal uses sambal. Demand is literally eternal.
  • Artisan/premium sambal growing fast (vs Indofood mass market). TikTok has made "sambal viral" a real category.
  • COGS is extremely low: chili, shallot, garlic, oil = Rp 3–5K/jar. Sell for Rp 20–35K. Gross margin 70–80%.
  • You can code a brand website + automate Shopee/Tokopedia listings.
  • Sister in Jakarta = HUGE distribution channel. Jakarta market pays premium for "authentic Solo/Jawa recipe" sambal.
  • No cold chain if properly bottled with preservative (shelf life 3–6 months).
  • Solo's Javanese culinary identity = authentic story = brand premium.
Why It's #2, Not #1
  • Requires PIRT certification (SPP-PIRT from Dinkes) to sell on Shopee/Tokopedia nationwide. ~1–3 months to obtain.
  • Raw material (chili prices) volatile — Rp 30K/kg normal, can spike to Rp 80–120K during shortage. Kills margin.
  • Competitive at scale — national brands (Sambal Bu Rudy, ABC) have distribution moats.
  • Quality consistency harder than liquid detergent — recipe ratios, moisture control, color consistency need training.
  • More food safety regulation than cleaning products.

30-Day Rapid Launch Plan

Day 1–3
Validate & Decide
You and father: walk through the house, map production space. Decide: 1 room for liquid mixing, 1 area for packaging. Call 3 kost operators nearby and ask "beli sabun cuci + deterjen di mana? Berapa per bulan?" — validate demand in 48 hours.
Day 4–7
Source Raw Materials + Equipment
Father visits Pasar Jongke / distributor bahan kimia Solo. Buy: SLS/LABS (surfactant), NaCl, fragrance, citric acid, preservative, 50L mixing container ×2, filling pump, sample bottles (1L ×50, 5L ×20). Target total spend: Rp 8–12M. Vibe code: brand name + label design in Canva.
Day 8–14
First Production Batch + Internal Quality Test
Produce first 100L sabun cuci piring + 100L deterjen cair. Test in your own kosts — give free samples to 10 kost residents. Collect feedback. Adjust fragrance/viscosity. Register NIB through OSS (oss.go.id) — takes 1 business day, free. This unlocks legal selling.
Day 15–21
First B2B Sales (Father's Job)
Father visits 10 kost operators in Laweyan with samples + price sheet. Target: 5 signed monthly supply agreements. Offer: sabun cuci piring Rp 9K/L (vs Sunlight Rp 12–15K retail), deterjen Rp 8K/L (vs Rinso bulk). "Harga langsung dari pabrik." Close 3–5 accounts = Rp 2–4M/month locked in.
Day 22–30
Launch Digital Presence (Your Job)
Open Shopee + Tokopedia store. List products with good photos (kost residents as lifestyle models). Set up WhatsApp Business catalog. Build simple 1-page ordering website (vibe code). Shoot first TikTok: "Produksi sabun cair dari rumah di Solo — UMKM lokal." Post 3 TikToks. Target: first 20 online orders in Week 4.
🏁
End of Month 1 target: Rp 5–8M revenue. 3–8 B2B accounts. 20–50 online orders. Production system running. Father comfortable with ops. You comfortable with digital. By month 3, target Rp 15–25M/month. By month 6, decide whether to add sambal line or double down on cleaning products based on what's working.
