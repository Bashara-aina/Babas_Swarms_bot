Strategies to Achieve 80–95% Occupancy

📱

1. List on OTA & Aggregator Platforms

Register both properties on Mamikos, Rukita, Sewakost, and OLX. D'Paragon's high pricing is partly sustained by Booking.com/Traveloka/Agoda visibility. Even being on Mamikos alone can add 4–6 monthly leads per month per property. Cost is low or free to list.

📅

2. Long-Stay Loyalty Discount

Offer a 5–10% discount for 3-month+ contracts paid upfront. This locks in occupancy at the cost of a small margin. Monthly contract tenants are more stable than daily/weekly. Advertise "bayar 3 bulan, hemat 1 bulan" type promotions via WhatsApp broadcast.

🐾

3. Amplify the Pet-Friendly Niche

You are one of the very few pet-friendly kost in Laweyan. Competitors do not offer this. Market specifically to pet owners via Instagram, Facebook groups like "Pet Owner Solo", and TikTok. This niche audience pays premium and has low elasticity.

⚡

4. Short-Stay Yield — Fix Daily Rate Gap

Current daily rates (Rp 150–170K) are too close to monthly ÷ 30. Recommended daily rates (Rp 175–225K) create a proper incentive for monthly stays while generating higher yield from short-stay guests. D'Paragon charges Rp 375K/day — you have wide room to move up.

🌐

5. Instagram / TikTok Content Strategy

Your digital-first brand (online booking, digital contracts, QRIS) is a story in itself. Create short videos showing the WiFi speed, digital check-in process, pet-friendly rooms. Target young professionals and remote workers — a growing segment in Solo looking for exactly this.

🎯

6. Corporate / University Partnerships

Laweyan is near UMS, Sahid University, and several hospitals. Partner directly with university housing offices or hospital HR for bulk referrals. Offer a "corporate rate" 5% below list price for guaranteed referrals. This alone can fill Labuh Banyu's vacant rooms.

Key Findings & Risk Flags

Labuh Biru is already near target (79%). A moderate price increase of 12–17% is low risk here — demand is proven. Do not discount. Test the new pricing on rooms vacated next turnover.
Labuh Banyu's 56% occupancy is a visibility issue, not a price issue. The facilities are identical, prices are competitive. Listing on Mamikos/Rukita is the fastest fix. Avoid discounting — it signals low quality.
Rumah Labuh's closest rival (D'Paragon, <1 km away) charges Rp 2.9M/month — 60–150% more than you. You can raise prices significantly and still be the better-value option in the immediate neighbourhood.
WiFi Mesh 150 Mbps + Pet-Friendly + Digital Contract are genuine differentiators that competitors do not offer. These justify a 10–20% premium over similar-spec properties that lack these features.
Daily rates are underpriced relative to the market. Current Rp 150–170K/day is below even mid-range competitors like D'Cantika (Rp 200K/day) and far below D'Paragon (Rp 375K/day). Raising daily rates captures short-stay margin without affecting long-term tenants.
Risk: Aggressive price increases could displace existing long-term tenants. Recommended approach — grandfather existing tenants at current rates, apply new pricing only to new bookings or renewals. Phase increases over 2 rental cycles (~3–4 months).
