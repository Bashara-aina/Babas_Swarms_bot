Daily Operational Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Pre-Opening Checklist (14:00-16:00)

  • 15:00: Charcoal light, grill warm-up (30min needed). Check thermometer (should reach 200°C).
  • 15:15: Bumbu kuah prepared (reheated from concentrate, consistency check). Taste a spoonful (should match Day 1 baseline).
  • 15:30: Daging sapi prep: portion control (150g per tusuk ×8 = portions ready). Soak in bumbu bath 10min before grilling.
  • 15:45: Station setup: grill zone ready, kuah station ready, packaging station ready, serving station ready. Cash register test. Music on.
  • 15:55: Staff briefing: today's inventory, any issues from yesterday, customer service reminder (greeting at door). Review GoFood online orders.
  • 16:00: Doors open. GoFood shop status set to "OPEN".

During Service (16:00-23:00)

  • Dine-in Orders: Max 5min wait from order to grill. Grill time 8-12min per order (adjust flame if needed). Plate + serve with smile. Collect feedback (optional brief chat).
  • Delivery Orders (GoFood/Grab): Accept order immediately. Prepare (same as dine-in). Pack with kuah terpisah, label clear (customer name, order time). Partner pickup within 5min of completion. No delay!
  • Quality Check Every Order: Owner or trained staff taste-tests 1 sate per 10 orders (20% sampling). Rate on: bumbu saturation, kuah temp, sate doneness. Flag any off-taste for bumbu adjustment.
  • Inventory Monitor: Every 2 hours, count remaining daging portions. If 50% sold by 19:00, you're on pace (100 sate/day expected). If slower, OK (normal). If ahead of pace, great!
  • Customer Interaction: Greet all dine-in. Ask "first time?" if new face. Mention loyalty (5th order 20% off). Encourage review ("tag us on Instagram!").

End-of-Service (23:00-23:30)

  • Close GoFood/Grab shop (set to "CLOSED").
  • Count daily sales (target record: # of daging used = # of sate sold).
  • Leftover bumbu: refrigerate in container (reusable next day if stored properly, ≤24hr).
  • Clean grill, utensils, kitchen (food safety critical).
  • Close register, reconcile cash.
  • Brief owner debrief: any issues, customer feedback, tomorrow's prep notes.
