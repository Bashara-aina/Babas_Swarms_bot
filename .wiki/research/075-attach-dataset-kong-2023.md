# Summary

ATTACH Dataset provides annotated two-handed assembly actions for human action understanding. The dataset focuses on capturing fine-grained hand movements during assembly tasks, enabling research on hand pose estimation and two-handed action recognition in industrial settings.

## Key Contributions

1. **Two-Handed Focus**: Specializes in two-person/two-handed assembly actions
2. **Frame-Level Annotations**: Provides precise temporal annotations for assembly steps
3. **Industrial Application**: Designed for human-robot collaboration scenarios

## Relevance to POPW

POPW's pose estimation head could benefit from ATTACH's two-handed annotation style. The dataset complements Assembly101 by providing more focused hand-level annotations.
