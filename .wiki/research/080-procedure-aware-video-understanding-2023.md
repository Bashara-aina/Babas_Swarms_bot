# Summary

This paper presents a procedure-aware pretraining approach for instructional video understanding. The method learns to recognize procedural patterns in instructional videos, enabling better understanding of multi-step assembly and maintenance tasks.

## Key Contributions

1. **Procedural Pretraining**: Learns temporal structure of procedures
2. **Instructional Videos**: Leverages IKEA-type instructional content
3. **Multi-step Reasoning**: Captures dependencies between procedure steps

## Relevance to POPW

POPW's activity recognition head would benefit from procedure-aware pretraining, similar to how this paper learns from instructional video structure. The approach captures canonical action orderings useful for assembly tasks.
