# Summary

This paper explores using foundation models for automatic data augmentation in hand pose estimation tasks. The approach leverages pre-trained models to generate synthetic training samples with varied hand poses and viewpoints.

## Key Contributions

1. **Foundation Model Integration**: Uses vision foundation models for augmentation
2. **Pose Diversity**: Increases variability in hand pose training data
3. **Synthetic Data Generation**: Creates realistic hand pose variations
