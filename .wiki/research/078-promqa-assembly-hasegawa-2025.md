# Summary

ProMQA-Assembly is a multimodal procedural question answering dataset focused on assembly tasks. The dataset contains instructional videos with corresponding question-answer pairs that require understanding of procedural activities, object states, and temporal sequences in assembly processes.

## Key Contributions

1. **Multimodal QA**: Combines video, text, and structural queries
2. **Assembly Focus**: Procedural questions about assembly steps and object states
3. **Fine-grained Understanding**: Requires multi-step reasoning about assembly procedures
