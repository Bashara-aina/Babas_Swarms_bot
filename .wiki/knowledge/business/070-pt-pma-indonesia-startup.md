# PT PMA Indonesia Mendirikan Startup Digital 2024

## Why This Matters for cekwajar.id
If cekwajar.id ever seeks foreign investment or partners with international entities, it may need to convert to PT PMA structure. Understanding the requirements and recent changes is critical for future planning.

## Core Knowledge

### What is PT PMA?
PT PMA (Perusahaan Penanaman Modal Asing) = Foreign Investment Limited Liability Company in Indonesia. An entity established in Indonesia with capital sourced partially or fully from foreign investors.

### Recent 2025 Changes - Major Update!
**BKPM Regulation No. 5/2025** (February 2026):
- **Old minimum capital**: Rp 10,000,000,000 (IDR 10 billion = ~$625K)
- **New minimum capital**: Rp 2,500,000,000 (IDR 2.5 billion = ~$156K)
- **This reduction opens doors for startups and foreign investors**

### PT PMA Requirements (2024-2025)

| Requirement | Details |
|------------|---------|
| **Minimum Shareholders** | 2+ shareholders (can mix Indonesian and foreign) |
| **Minimum Capital** | Rp 2.5 billion (~$156K at 16,000 IDR/USD) |
| **KBLI Classification** | Must match the business activity code |
| **Director** | At least 1 director (can be foreign) |
| **Commissioner** | Indonesian company needs at least 1 commissioner |
| **Tax ID (NPWP)** | Required for company registration |

### Digital Startup-Specific KBLI Codes
- **62011**: Computer programming activities
- **62012**: Software development
- **62020**: Computer consultancy
- **63901**: Other information service activities (portals)

### Process to Establish PT PMA
1. **Investment Approval (BKPM)**: Submit application via online system
2. **Company Name Reservation**: Deed of Establishment drafted
3. **Notary**: Deed of Establishment by licensed notary
4. **Ministry of Law and Human Rights**: Legal entity approval
5. **NPWP**: Tax registration
6. **NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha)**: Business Identification Number
7. **Sector-specific licenses**: Depending on business type

### Documents Required
- Passport copies of shareholders
- Investment plan
- Company structure (board of directors, commissioners)
- Business activities description
- Proof of capital deposit

## Edge Cases and Common Mistakes
1. **Wrong KBLI**: Using incorrect business activity code causes licensing issues
2. **Minimum capital misunderstanding**: Must be fully paid-up capital, not just authorized
3. **Foreign-only board**: Need at least 1 Indonesian commissioner for most structures
4. **Skipping NIB**: Cannot operate without Business Identification Number
5. **Timing**: Process takes 4-8 weeks minimum with professional help
