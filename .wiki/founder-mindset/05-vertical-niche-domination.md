# Vertical Niche Domination — The Anti-Horizontal Trap

Source: Glean Playbook + Harvey AI + EvenUp patterns

## The Trap
Most builders try to build a horizontal agent that does everything.
Result: mediocre at everything, excellent at nothing.
Enterprises won't pay for mediocre.

## The Playbook
1. Pick ONE vertical with language-heavy, unstructured text pain
2. Build the ONE most painful workflow first
3. Fine-tune on domain-specific data → model becomes expert
4. Expand to adjacent workflows within the SAME vertical
5. Only go horizontal AFTER you own a vertical

## Examples
- Harvey AI → legal (contracts, due diligence, litigation prep)
- EvenUp → personal injury law specifically
- Freed → doctors specifically (not "healthcare")
- Tennr → healthcare fax reading specifically

## Applied to Legion / cekwajar.id
- cekwajar.id vertical: Indonesian labor law + salary verification
- First workflow: PPh 21 calculation + BPJS deduction
- Adjacent: UMR comparison, termination calculation
- Do NOT expand to general HR until salary verification is #1 in Indonesia

## The Moat
Domain expertise + customer data > code quality.
Anyone can copy code. Nobody can copy 10,000 Indonesian salary calculations.
