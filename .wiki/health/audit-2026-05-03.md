### P1 — HIGH (Fix Within 1 Week)

**4. SemanticCache Broken** (Sector 12 — Score: 8/10)
- HuggingFace token expired — cache silently fails
- `hf.ChatHubLLM` raises `InferenceEndpointError`
- **Impact:** Every semantic cache lookup falls through to full LLM call — increased latency + cost

**5. crewAI Sync Kickoff Blocking Event Loop** (Sector 14 — Score: 5/10)

- Under load this causes cascading failures in aiogram dispatcher

- **Fix:** Convert to fully async executor pattern or replace with langgraph equivalent

**6. Observation Hooks Never Emit** (Sector 10 — Score: 5/10)

- Observation capture is broken — no data flows into memory systems
- **Impact:** Memory systems are starving; mem0/langmem receive no signals
- **Fix:** Emit hooks from appropriate call sites in agent_core

**7. self_review Needs 50 Conversations** (Sector 9 — Score: 2/10)
- Self-review gate is 50 conversations — no conversation counter exists
- Will never trigger in current state
- **Impact:** Long-term reflection and refinement is impossible
