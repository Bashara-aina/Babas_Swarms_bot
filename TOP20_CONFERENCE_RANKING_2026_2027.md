## Scoring Methodology

Each conference scored 0-10 across 5 weighted dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Scope Match | 30% | How many POPW pillars does it cover? Are they explicit tracks? |
| Deadline Feasibility | 20% | Is deadline confirmed? How much prep time? |
| Travel Logistics | 20% | Flight time, visa, cost from Tokyo |
| Venue Prestige | 15% | Publisher (IEEE/ACM/Springer), ranking, indexing |
| Best Paper Potential | 15% | Award categories, competitiveness, uniqueness of paper |

**TOTAL = weighted sum, cap at 100.**
