# Andrej Karpathy

## TL;DR
Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher formerly at Tesla Autopilot and OpenAI, now known for educational content and LLM knowledge base patterns.

## Background

- **Former positions**:
  - Director of AI at Tesla Autopilot
  - Research Scientist at OpenAI
  - Lecturer at Stanford (CS231n)

- **Current work**:
  - Educational content on YouTube/Twitter
  - LLM knowledge base systems
  - Open source projects
